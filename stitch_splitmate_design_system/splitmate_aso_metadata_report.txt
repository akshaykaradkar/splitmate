## 3. Comprehensive Long Description (App Store & Google Play)

Split group bills without the chaos, awkward IOUs, or mystery pennies. SplitMate brings calm, mathematical precision, and an organic tactile design to group travel, roommate rent, dinner tabs, and shared events.

Whether you're organizing a weekend ski cabin, splitting groceries with housemates, or divvying up a 14-item restaurant receipt with tax and tip, SplitMate ensures every participant pays exactly what they owe—down to the exact cent.

### 🌿 WHY SPLITMATE IS DIFFERENT

• **Instant Guest Mode (Zero Signup Required)**: 
Start splitting in under 5 seconds. No passwords, no phone numbers, no forced social logins. SplitMate operates offline-first with an encrypted on-device SQLite database, keeping your personal spending private by default.

• **Live Receipt Claim & Remainder Engine**:
Snap a receipt or enter items directly. Each friend claims their items, while shared plates, service charges, local tax, and tip multipliers are automatically distributed proportionally. Our deterministic remainder engine attributes any sub-cent rounding differences so bills always balance with zero penny drift.

• **Greedy Debt Simplification**:
Say goodbye to messy circular debt chains where A owes B, B owes C, and C owes A. SplitMate’s Greedy Min-Cash Flow algorithm reduces complex multi-person webs into the absolute minimum number of direct transfers (e.g., turning 10 confusing peer transactions into just 2 clean payments).

• **Tactile, Calming Interface**:
Inspired by organic minimalism and soft earth-tone aesthetics, SplitMate replaces intimidating, cluttered financial spreadsheets with warm pastel bento summaries, tactile calculator keypads, and fluid spring micro-interactions.

• **Instant 1-Tap Settlements**:
Settle balances seamlessly with integrated peer-to-peer deep links for Venmo, Cash App, and bank transfers, complete with auto-reconciled ledger receipts.

• **Multi-Currency & International Travel Ready**:
Full support for USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), CAD ($), CHF (Fr), and more. Track travel expenses offline on planes, off-grid cabins, or abroad without roaming fees.

### ✨ KEY FEATURES AT A GLANCE
- **Itemized Live Receipt Splitting**: Custom tax & tip multipliers with real-time participant progress bars.
- **Smart Split Strategies**: Equal splits, exact dollar amounts, percentages, or custom weighted shares.
- **Multi-Group Management**: Keep vacation trips, apartment rent, couples spending, and dinner clubs organized separately.
- **Audit Footprint & History**: Immutable ledger trail showing who paid, who claimed, and when transactions settled.
- **Data Sovereignty**: Export complete ledger history to CSV or JSON anytime. Zero ad trackers.
