# Aurora Rubric - Android Motion Compliance

1.  [ID: aurora_energy_max_one] If an Aurora energy surface is present, does the screen contain a maximum of ONE active energy element at any given time? [Metric type: Adherence] [Weight: 10]

2.  [ID: aurora_energy_subtle] Does the Aurora energy surface treatment appear subtle and balanced, without visually overwhelming or dominating the screen experience? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]

3.  [ID: aurora_energy_signal_ai] Is the Aurora energy effect applied exclusively to signal active AI capability and assist the user's understanding that AI is present? [Metric type: Adherence] [Weight: 10]

4.  [ID: aurora_small_container_tonal_fill] If an Aurora energy surface is applied to a small container with a blurred (uncontained) edge, does the container feature a low-contrast tonal fill using `--droid-sys-color-tertiary-container` (light: `#c4eed0`, dark: `#0f5223`) or `--droid-sys-color-secondary-container` (light: `#c2e7ff`, dark: `#004a77`) rather than a high-contrast fill? [Requires Code Inspection] [Metric type: Adherence] [Weight: 3]

5.  [ID: aurora_large_container_sharp_edge] If an Aurora energy surface is applied to a large container, does the container maintain a crisp, sharp outer boundary edge conforming to `--droid-sys-shape-corner-large` (16px) or `--droid-sys-shape-corner-extra-large` (28px)? [Requires Code Inspection] [Metric type: Adherence] [Weight: 3]

6.  [ID: aurora_energy_extend_edges] Does the active Aurora energy effect extend fully to the defined outer edges of its parent component or container? [Requires Code Inspection] [Metric type: Adherence] [Weight: 3]

7.  [ID: aurora_4c_brand_ai_moments] If a 4C Aurora energy variant is active, is its application restricted ONLY to signature, high-impact brand-AI moments? [Metric type: Adherence] [Weight: 10]

8.  [ID: aurora_neural_gemini_only] If a Neural Aurora energy variant is present, is its usage restricted exclusively to Gemini-branded intelligence features? [Metric type: Adherence] [Weight: 10]

9.  [ID: aurora_text_icon_contrast] Does text and icon content layered over or adjacent to an active Aurora energy surface maintain high-contrast legibility without being obscured or visually interfered with? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]

10. [ID: aurora_media_content_clear] Does media content (such as photos, videos, or illustrations) remain clear and unobscured by overlapping Aurora energy surfaces? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]

11. [ID: aurora_motion_breathing_cycle] If the Aurora energy surface is in a thinking, processing, or idle breathing state, does it animate dynamically using slow transition durations like `--droid-sys-motion-duration-800` (800ms) or `--droid-sys-motion-duration-900` (900ms) paired with `--droid-sys-motion-easing-linear` or `--droid-sys-motion-easing-emphasized`? [Requires Code Inspection] [Metric type: Adherence] [Weight: 5]
