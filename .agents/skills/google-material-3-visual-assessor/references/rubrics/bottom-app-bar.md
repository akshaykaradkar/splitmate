# Bottom App Bar Rubric

1.  [ID: bottom_app_bar_container_shape] Does the bottom app bar either stretch full-width (docked) or sit as a pill-shaped floating bar? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 3]

2.  [ID: bottom_app_bar_button_target_size] Do action buttons inside the bottom app bar appear large enough to touch easily (minimum 48x48dp touch target size)? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 3]

3.  [ID: bottom_app_bar_floating_drop_shadow] Does a floating bottom app bar use a drop shadow or elevation level (`--md-sys-elevation-level1` or higher) to elevate it from the background? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 3]

4.  [ID: bottom_app_bar_simultaneous_nav] Are bottom app bars and bottom navigation bars avoided from appearing simultaneously on the same screen? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]
