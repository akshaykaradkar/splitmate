# Segmented Button

1.  [ID: segmented_button_usage] Are segmented buttons used to toggle between
    complementary views or settings (e.g., Day/Week/Month), and do they consist of 2 to 5 segments? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 3]

2.  [ID: segmented_button_inner_segments_dividers] Are the inner segments
    separated by clean internal dividing lines of 1dp width (using `--md-sys-color-outline` or `--md-sys-color-outline-variant`)? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 3]

3.  [ID: segmented_button_content_centered] Are all text labels (written in sentence case) and associated
    icons centered perfectly within their respective segment? [Metric type: Hygiene]
    [Weight: 1]

4.  [ID: segmented_button_no_drop_shadow] Does the container stay flat without a
    drop shadow for elevation (level 0 elevation)? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 3]
