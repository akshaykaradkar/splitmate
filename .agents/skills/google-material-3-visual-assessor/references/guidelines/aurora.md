# Aurora - Universal AI Detection Guide & GM3 Specifications

This reference provides universal visual heuristics for detecting Aurora components (AI energy surfaces and intelligence elements) across design systems and platforms, followed by Google Material 3 (GM3) specifications for compliance auditing.

## Part 1: Universal AI Detection Heuristics

**Aurora** represents Google's design language for the AI era, featuring an updated metaphor of "energy" to represent the underlying intelligence powering Google's products. Visual models must detect Aurora elements based on their distinct energy gradients, luminous borders, and conversational container features.

### Key Visual & Geometric Heuristics

-   **Aurora Energy Surface**: Iridescent, luminous, or multi-colored gradient energy treatments applied to component boundaries, containers, or background surfaces to indicate active AI presence, processing, or generation.
-   **Aurora Chat Bubble**: Conversational message containers featuring AI energy accents, gradient backgrounds, or distinct intelligence styling.
-   **Aurora Prompt Field**: Text entry containers specifically designed for AI prompt input, often bordered or glowing with Aurora gradient effects.
-   **Aurora Response Carousel**: A horizontal swipeable container presenting multiple AI-generated responses, recommendations, or suggestions.
-   **Aurora Response List**: A vertical container grouping AI response elements or generated text blocks.
-   **Aurora Suggestions**: Interactive suggestion chips or pill containers offering recommended follow-up prompts or AI actions.

## Part 2: GM3 Specifications & Compliance Auditing

When conducting a Google Material 3 (GM3) adherence audit for Aurora elements, evaluate against the following strict brand and interface standards:

### GM3 Visual & Behavioral Rules

-   **Energy Surface Cardinality**: At most **ONE** active Aurora energy surface or element may be present on a screen at any given time to prevent visual noise.
-   **AI Signal Exclusivity**: Aurora energy effects must be applied exclusively to signal active AI capabilities or active system-to-user collaboration.
-   **Container Edges & Fills**:
    -   *Small Containers*: Blurred (uncontained) edge energy surfaces must use low-contrast tonal fills rather than high-contrast fills to maintain layout hierarchy.
    -   *Large Containers*: Large energy containers must maintain crisp, sharp outer boundary edges.
    -   *Parent Edge Extension*: Energy effects must extend fully to parent container boundaries.
-   **Variant Specifications**:
    -   *Four-color (4C) Brand Gradient*: The official full-color gradient representing Google's most iconic brand moments. It must always be used with blur to soften transitions between the core colors and avoid visual banding. The embedded sequence and values must never be modified, and transitional stops must not be used out of context. Restricted to signature, high-impact brand-AI moments.
    -   *Neural Aurora Variant*: A specific technical-grade gradient representing raw intelligence. Restricted exclusively to Gemini-branded intelligence features.
-   **Content Legibility**: Text, icons, and media layered over or adjacent to Aurora energy surfaces must maintain high-contrast legibility without interference. Ensure text and overlay icons have proper contrast (minimum 4.5:1 for standard text, 3:1 for large text/icons).
-   **Media Content Integrity**: Media content (photos, videos, or illustrations) must remain clear and completely unobscured by overlapping Aurora energy surfaces.
