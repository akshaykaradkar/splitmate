# Carousel

1.  [ID: carousel_usage_limit] Is the carousel limited to a maximum of 5 slides
    to present crucial onboarding or newly available feature announcements? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]

2.  [ID: carousel_close_button] Is a close "X" icon button persistently present
    and focusable in the upper right corner of the carousel container to allow
    dismissal? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 8]

3.  [ID: carousel_dialog_shape_corners] Does the modal dialog card container comply
    with the Elements GM3 specification: Extra Large corner radius of 28dp? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]

4.  [ID: carousel_text_brevity] Is the copy highly concise, with titles under
    45 characters (single-line preferred) and body text under 300 characters? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]

5.  [ID: carousel_navigation_arrows] Are "Next" and "Previous" directional icon
    buttons provided to ensure efficient keyboard functionality and visual aid? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 8]

6.  [ID: carousel_dots_non_interactive] Are pagination dots serving strictly as
    visual guidance on progress and explicitly made non-interactive to prevent
    accidental triggers? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]

7.  [ID: carousel_button_rules] Is there a maximum of two action buttons in the
    footer, and do their labels clearly describe the destination (avoiding generic
    "Learn more")? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 8]

8.  [ID: carousel_autorotation_rules] If the carousel automatically rotates and
    lasts more than 5 seconds, does it offer a pause/stop/hide option, and is
    autorotation completely disabled on mobile/small screens? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]

9.  [ID: carousel_target_sizes] Do all interactive targets (arrows, buttons, close)
    meet the minimum 48x48px target size for accessibility? [Metric type: Hygiene] [Weight: 5]
