## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines collaborative group finance by pairing the mathematical precision of the Remainder Engine with a deeply calming, organic aesthetic inspired by mindful expense trackers. Rather than leaning into corporate blue grids or cold fintech neons, it establishes a serene, earth-toned atmosphere designed to defuse money-induced friction among friends, housemates, and travel companions.

### Core Philosophy & Visual Voice
- **Organic Tactility:** Generous, cloud-soft curved containers (24px to 32px radii), pill badges, and playful wavy dual-tone partitions evoke physical paper craft and calm ceramics.
- **Low-Arousal Financial Feedback:** High-stress moments—such as bill split discrepancies, leftover balances, and debts—are rendered in gentle, readable terracotta, warm amber, and sage tints rather than aggressive alarms.
- **Material 3 Expressive Harmonization:** Fluid tonal surfaces with ambient light diffusion replace stiff structural drop shadows. Component transitions and keypad interactions react with subtle spring physics and natural damping.

## Colors

The palette draws entirely from natural minerals, field grains, and sun-warmed earth. It establishes a comforting, light-mode-first environment where numerical balances appear clear, legible, and unhurried.

### Canvas & Surface Structure
- **Canvas Base (`#fdfbf7`):** The primary view background layer. Warm, creamy, and gentle on the eyes.
- **Canvas Warm (`#f7f3ec`):** Grouping modules, bottom sheet foundations, and input keypad decks.
- **Canvas Sunken (`#f2ede4`):** Recessed wells, breakdown logs, and itemized entry bays.
- **Surface White (`#ffffff`):** Crisp, high-contrast cards providing immediate lift for key balance modules.

### Functional Split & Remainder Semantics
- **Reconciled & Settle (`sage-soft` `#d7e8b6` / `sage-deep` `#8bb85a`):** Communicates complete reconciliation, settled shares, and balanced ledger lines without harsh electric greens. Text sits in contrasting `sage-dark` (`#365314`).
- **Unassigned Remainder (`peach-tint` `#fed8c8` / `butter-remainder` `#fef08a`):** Marks unallocated sums pending user distribution. Paired with readable warm tones (`#713f12`), guiding action without triggering cognitive urgency.
- **Over-Claim Discrepancy (`coral-overclaim` `#fecdd3`):** Warns against excess allocation with a gentle coral blush and deep ruby lettering (`coral-overclaim-text` `#881337`).

### Multi-Participant Palette
Split participants receive soft pastel identifications across avatars, claim pills, and progress tracks:
- **Participant 1:** Sage Leaf (`#b8d98d`)
- **Participant 2:** Terracotta Warmth (`#fbc3b5`)
- **Participant 3:** Periwinkle Mist (`#c7d2fe`)
- **Participant 4:** Chamomile Cream (`#fef08a`)
- **Participant 5:** Rose Clay (`#f5b5cc`)
- **Participant 6:** Muted Sky (`#bae6fd`)

## Typography

The typography leverages a singular, high-clarity geometric typeface with softened terminals and friendly humanist proportions. This gives the interface an approachable, lifestyle-first demeanor while preserving the structural precision necessary for mathematical inputs.

### Tabular Formatting Directive
To prevent jitters during real-time split arithmetic and balance tally updates, all currency amounts, decimal inputs, percentages, date counters, and keypad numerals must apply tabular numeric open-type features:
```css
font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "ss01" 1;
font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;
```

### Hierarchy & Style Rules
- **Display Numerals:** Big, confident currency figures (e.g. `display-large` and `display-medium`) use tight letter spacing with prominent, rounded currency symbols.
- **Sentence Case Only:** All interactive actions, pill chips, section titles, and status alerts strictly use sentence case. Capitalize only proper nouns and leading words.
- **Warm Contrast:** Primary copy renders in soft charcoal `#23201e` instead of pitch black, avoiding visual harshness against the pastel backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a breathable rhythm rooted in a 4px/8px modular scale, maintaining open negative space to produce a relaxed, uncluttered feel.

### Form Factors
- **Handheld Mobile (< 600dp):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins. Vertical flow stacks large interactive hero balance modules above scrollable expense feeds, pinned by a thumb-accessible floating keypad or action island.
- **Tablet / Split Screen (600dp – 900dp):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters. Left side accommodates the bill receipt split breakdown, while the right displays the dynamic participant settlement matrix.
- **Desktop (> 900dp):** 12-column layout centered with a maximum container width of `1120px` to maintain focused, intimate proportions.

### Touch Target Mandate
All interactive components—including the custom numeric keypad triggers, avatar pill selectors, date chips, and delete badges—must maintain a bounding touch area of at least 48px × 48px to support effortless single-handed mobile tapping.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered warm surfaces, gentle background tones, and soft tinted ambient contact shadows, avoiding artificial black drop shadows entirely.

### Surface Stacking Tiers
- **Canvas (Level 0):** `#fdfbf7` base screen.
- **Soft Wells (Level 1):** `#f7f3ec` with a hairline tinted border (`1px solid #ebe4d8`) for segmented rows, inactive chips, and calendar date rings.
- **Floating Organic Cards (Level 2):** `#ffffff` or pastel fill (`#d7e8b6`, `#fed8c8`, `#d9dffc`) elevated with an ambient glow:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(99, 94, 89, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(99, 94, 89, 0.03);`
- **Raised Interactive Islands & Keypads (Level 3):** `#ffffff` bottom action sheets and elevated toolbars:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(54, 50, 47, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(54, 50, 47, 0.04);`

### Contact Physics
Interactive buttons and keypad cells feature elastic tactile feedback. On press, cards compress smoothly with a subtle scale transformation (`scale(0.97)`) using an ease-out spring curve (200ms duration).

## Shapes

The design system's signature identity relies on smooth, friendly rounded geometry. Sharp corners are entirely absent.

### Corner Radius Hierarchy
- **Extra Large / Main Cards (28px to 32px):** Hero net-balance modules, dual-tone split cards, and primary content containers.
- **Large / Nested Cards (20px to 24px):** Sub-budget cards, category summary rows, keypad housing, and modal dialogs.
- **Medium / Controls (14px to 16px):** Keypad numeric buttons, input fields, and action buttons.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Top app-bar date indicators, persona chips, tag badges, and bottom floating action pills.

### Organic Split Silhouette
Hero overview cards employ a two-tone layout divided by an organic asymmetric wave curve (SVG path fill) dividing remaining vs. allocated funds, directly capturing the distinctive wavy container treatment seen in mindful budget trackers.

## Components

### 1. Dual-Tone Organic Hero Split Card
- **Structure:** 28px or 32px rounded card featuring an organic vertical or horizontal wavy division separating two soft pastel tones (e.g. Sage `#d7e8b6` on the left or top, Warm Cream `#f7f3ec` on the right or bottom).
- **Content:** The primary portion features prominent display numerals (`headline-large` or `display-medium`) for total settled or available funds. The adjacent section displays remaining split requirements or target balances.
- **Micro-labels:** Crisp, low-contrast text in `label-small` or `body-small` (`#635e59`) positioned in generous negative space.

### 2. Remainder Engine Progress Bar
- **Form:** A 16px high pill-shaped bar with smooth continuous bounds.
- **Fill Partition:** Proportional segment fills correspond to each participant's allocated share using their specific pastel token.
- **Unsettled Remainder:** Rendered via an active pastel warning tint (`#fed8c8` or `#fef08a`). Clicking the bar triggers a quick-action pill: *"Split remainder equally"*.
- **Balanced State:** Transitions seamlessly to unified `#b8d98d` sage fill when the split matches 100% of the bill total.

### 3. Minimalist Organic Keypad
- **Housing:** Recessed deck styled in `#f7f3ec` with 28px top radii.
- **Key Cells:** Rounded rectangles (16px radius) in `#ffffff`, sized at a minimum of 64px width × 54px height with soft charcoal text (`headline-small`).
- **Action / Enter Key:** High-contrast organic accent button styled in warm peach or terracotta (`#f59f89` or `#fed8c8`) with matching dark lettering (`#7c2d12`).
- **Backspace & Operators:** Clean icon-only glyphs set on low-contrast surface cells.

### 4. Persona Switcher & Category Pills
- **Badge Anatomy:** Pill geometry (`9999px`), 36px to 40px height, inset padding `0.375rem 1rem`.
- **Resting State:** `#f2ede4` canvas well with `#23201e` typography.
- **Active Selection:** Fills with vibrant category pastel (e.g. `#d9dffc` for utilities, `#fed8c8` for dining, `#d7e8b6` for general group claims) paired with an understated 1.5px matching border ring.

### 5. Expense Feed Item Cards
- **Card Geometry:** 20px rounded card in `#ffffff` with a subtle 1px border (`#ebe4d8`).
- **Left Wing:** 44px circular or 14px rounded pastel avatar chip indicating category or paying participant.
- **Center Wing:** Title in `title-medium` with date/split ratio subtext in `body-small` (`#635e59`).
- **Right Wing:** Tabular bold amount in `title-medium` (`#23201e`), paired with a mini pill tag confirming status (*"Settled"*, *"Owed €12"*, or *"Pending"*).

### 6. Outlined Input Fields
- **Container:** 16px corner radius, minimum height 52px, light cream fill (`#ffffff`).
- **Border:** 1.5px solid `#ebe4d8`, shifting to soft sage (`#8bb85a`) or warm terracotta on focus.
- **Typography:** Value input renders in tabular `title-medium`, with soft secondary hint text in `#948e85`.

### 7. Calendar & Date Selector Pills
- **Compact Layout:** Horizontal scroll rail of day pills (34px × 48px, 16px radius).
- **Active State:** Soft sage container (`#d7e8b6`) with dark olive numeral (`#365314`), conveying a mindful daily spending cadence.
